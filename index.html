Hi, I'm Rohit! 👋

I created BoardQuest with the vision of solving two major challenges faced by CBSE Class 10 board exam aspirants: monotonous textbook revision and silent knowledge gaps.

By combining a full 3D dynamic board game built with Three.js, a non-repeating question engine, and an integrated Gemini Personal AI Tutor, BoardQuest helps students master all 6 board subjects (Maths, Science, SST, English, Hindi, and IT 402) while tracking real-time diagnostic performance.

BoardQuest Core Features

Designed for comprehensive syllabus completion and high board performance.

🎲

Interactive 3D Board

Features over 100+ board chapters mapped in a continuous 3D spiral scene with dynamic lighting matching each subject theme.

⚡

Non-Repeating Engine

Ensures questions never repeat across quizzes by dynamically parametrizing formulas and scenarios per chapter.

🔍

Deep Diagnostic Analysis

Incorrect answers trigger targeted topic breakdowns, highlighting exact weak concepts for revision.

📚

NCERT & PW Notes

Includes high-yield revision notes, key formulas, board glossary terms, and structured 3-column study library PDFs.

✨

Gemini AI Teacher

Integrated AI chat assistant to explain concepts, generate competency questions, and draft 7-day revision schedules.

⏱️

Inbuilt Exam Timer

Practice under realistic board conditions with toggleable 3-hour exam countdowns, test timers, and 25-min Pomodoro modes.
