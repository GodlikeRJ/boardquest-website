📘 Science Quick Revision: Chemical Reactions

  • Combination Reaction: Two reactants combine to form a single product ($A + B \rightarrow AB$).
  • Decomposition Reaction: A single compound breaks down into simpler substances ($AB \rightarrow A + B$).
  • Displacement Reaction: A more reactive element displaces a less reactive element from its salt solution.
